The Problem

Leadership gets lonely at the level you're operating.

Most owners are making six- and seven-figure calls without a board, without a partner who's seen it before, and without anyone in the room who isn't a direct report. The higher you climb, the fewer people can actually tell you the truth.

What It Is

A curated room of people who get the weight of the work.

Every group is hand-matched for stage and industry fit, then facilitated by someone trained to keep the conversation honest — not comfortable.

How It Works

Support doesn't stop when the session ends.

Members stay connected between sessions over a private group thread, come together annually for a retreat, and share dinners throughout the year. Each session centers two members at a time — "Center Stage" — so every problem gets real depth instead of a five-minute check-in.
